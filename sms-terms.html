Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. The program

Biddle Systems operates an SMS service ("Jarvis Assistant") on the toll-free number +1 (855) 677-5360. The service sends conversational replies from an AI assistant, along with alerts and notifications that the recipient has asked to receive. It is not a marketing or promotional program, and Biddle Systems does not send advertising on this number.

2. Who can participate

The program is limited to the account owner and contacts the account owner has explicitly approved. You must be the subscriber of the phone number you enroll, or have the subscriber's permission, and you must be at least 18 years old.

3. Opting in

Text START to +1 (855) 677-5360 to join. SUBSCRIBE, JOIN, YES and UNSTOP are also accepted. You will receive one confirmation message. By opting in you give express consent to receive text messages at the number you used. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

4. Message frequency and cost

Message frequency is recurring and varies — the assistant replies when you message it, and sends only the alerts you have requested. Message and data rates may apply according to your plan with your mobile carrier. Biddle Systems does not charge for messages.

5. Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to cancel. STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END and QUIT have the same effect. You will receive a single confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages after that. To rejoin, text START again.

6. Help

Reply HELP for information, or contact mwbiddle1@gmail.com.

7. Carriers and delivery

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is subject to effective transmission by your mobile carrier and is outside Biddle Systems' control. Supported carriers may change without notice.

8. Automated replies

Replies are generated by an AI assistant and may be inaccurate or incomplete. They are provided for convenience and should not be relied on as professional, legal, financial or medical advice. Do not send sensitive information such as payment card numbers, government identification numbers, or passwords by text.

9. Privacy

Message content and phone numbers are handled as described in our Privacy Policy. Biddle Systems does not sell or rent phone numbers or message content, and does not share mobile information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
