Jarvis Assistant — SMS Program

Jarvis is a private AI assistant operated by Biddle Systems. It answers text messages from the account owner and a small list of approved contacts, and sends them alerts they have asked for. This page explains how to join, what you will receive, and how to stop at any time.

How to opt in

Text START to

+1 (855) 677-5360

By texting START to the number above, you are giving express consent to receive text messages from Biddle Systems. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. You may cancel at any time by replying STOP.

SUBSCRIBE, JOIN, YES and UNSTOP are also accepted as opt-in keywords.
